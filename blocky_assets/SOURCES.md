## Style notes common to all three
- Vertical 9:16 at 60 fps, one shot every 1–3 s, with fast push-ins and close-ups on faces.
- Captions: bold rounded sans with a black outline. Either the key word is bright green (#3CE63C) or all words are
  white, one word at a time (`textures/signs/caption_example_*.png`).
- Lighting looks: bright daytime, purple dusk, deep-blue night with warm emissive windows, grey storm with rain,
  and golden sunrise above the clouds.
